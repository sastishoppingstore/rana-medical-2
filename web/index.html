A few helpful answers

Questions, answered.

What are the OPD timings?

OPD schedules vary by department and doctor. Please contact the hospital to confirm current timings before visiting.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this page or call 0324-6627054. Online booking is a demonstration workflow in this preview.

How does the digital token work?

A demo token is generated after a successful booking. At the hospital, please confirm your token with reception.

Can I get laboratory home sampling?

Please contact the hospital to confirm whether home sample collection is currently available in your area.

How can I access my lab report?

Use the secure portal details provided by the hospital. The public sample lookup here only demonstrates reference verification.

How do I get emergency admission?

For urgent assistance call 0324-6627054 or visit the emergency department. Call emergency services if you are in immediate danger.

What payment methods are accepted?

Please contact reception for current accepted payment methods and billing information.

How can I contact the hospital?

Call 0324-6627054 or message via WhatsApp at +92 324 6627054.
